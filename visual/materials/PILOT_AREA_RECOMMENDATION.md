# V02 pilot area recommendation

## Recommendation: Temple of Time main hall

Temple of Time is the best single pilot for a registry proof of concept: recognizable stone architecture, pillars, emblem/pedestal elements, controlled lighting and enough repeated surfaces to test stable material identity. It offers a useful mix of opaque candidates while avoiding a pilot dominated by UI or transparent effects.

## Alternatives considered

- Hyrule Castle: good stone, but smaller/less repeatable material coverage.
- Kakariko Village: useful wood/stone mix, but more outdoor lighting and foliage variability.
- Water Temple: valuable water test, but water ordering/transparency makes it a poor first registry pilot.

## V02 boundary

Capture and classify a small set of Temple of Time draws first. Preserve original albedo, UVs, combiner, alpha, wrap and order. Begin with classic vs enhanced constants only. Do not create normals, upscale assets or alter geometry until the registry identity and fallback are proven.
